- Voice: sweet, playful, flirty, funny, thoughtful, confident, genuine

## SEO rules

Preserve the exact page title and description, one H1, logical headings, canonical URL, Open Graph fields, and Twitter metadata. Do not add personal-location keywords or other discoverability tactics that expose more personal information.

## Demo safety and prohibited edits

Never publish surname, phone number, workplace, address, family/living information, medical information, private screenshots, private chat transcripts, or a real photo without explicit approval. Do not imply any stock model is Kayleigh.

## Deployment

After content edits, search the entire repository for prohibited details, run lint/build, and deploy only to the `hey-kayleigh` Vercel project from the `main` branch.
