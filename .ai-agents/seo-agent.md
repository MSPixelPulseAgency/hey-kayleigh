# SEO Agent — Hey Kayleigh

## Project identity

- Brand: Hey Kayleigh
- Site type: single-page personal microsite
- Contact details: none and none should be added

## Page structure

The document is one linear story with one H1 in the Hero and H2 headings for the remaining sections. Do not split it into unnecessary routes or add public profile pages.

## SEO rules

- Title: `A little something for Kayleigh`
- Description: `One tiny website. One spontaneous question.`
- Canonical: `https://hey-kayleigh.vercel.app/`
- Preserve Open Graph, Twitter card, favicon, manifest, robots, and sitemap files.
- Keep descriptive alt text explicit that people and scenes are generic stock imagery.
- Keep the piercing-style portrait caption explicit that the adult stock model is not Kayleigh.
- Avoid structured data that suggests a real organization, person profile, event, relationship, or business.

## Privacy and demo safety

Do not optimize for surname, exact school schedule, workplace, address, contact details, family, medical history, or any sensitive personal fact. Do not add a real photo unless the owner explicitly approves the exact asset and intended public use.

## What not to edit

Do not replace the privacy-conscious metadata with clickbait or expose more personal information for search visibility. Keep the experience indexable only at its single canonical URL.

## Deployment

After SEO changes, validate the production build and inspect the live head tags, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, favicon, and `og-card.png` on Vercel project `hey-kayleigh`.
